Shipping

Last updated October 31, 2026

Who we sell to

All orders are for adults 21 and over. By ordering you confirm you are 21 or older. We may ask for proof of age, and orders may need an adult (21+) signature on delivery. We cancel and refund any order we can't verify.

Where we ship

We ship within the United States. We don't ship to states or localities where herbal smoking products are restricted. If we can't ship to your address, we'll cancel and fully refund the order.

Processing time

Orders ship within 1–3 business days. You'll get an email with tracking once your order is on its way.

Shipping cost and delivery time

Shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay. Most orders arrive within 3–7 business days after they ship.

Local pickup and Brooklyn delivery

You can also pick up at our Brooklyn market stands or ask for local delivery in Brooklyn. We'll confirm the details by email.

Lost or damaged packages

If your order arrives damaged or doesn't arrive, email us within 7 days of the expected delivery date with your order number and a photo (if damaged). We'll replace it or refund you.
