Refunds & Returns

Last updated October 31, 2026

Unopened packs

You can return unopened, sealed packs within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price. Email us first so we can send return instructions. Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item was damaged or wrong.

Opened packs

Because these are consumable products, we can't accept returns of opened packs. If something is wrong with your pack, such as damaged or poorly rolled cigarettes, email us with a photo and we'll make it right with a replacement or refund.

Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days after we receive the return or approve the claim.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order for a full refund any time before it ships. Email us as soon as possible.
